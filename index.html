Resource Fair Planning Guide

The Crater Health District (CHD), in partnership with local governments, schools, healthcare providers, and community-based organizations, puts on large-scale community health and resource fairs across the localities of Petersburg, Hopewell, Greensville/Emporia, Surry, Dinwiddie, Sussex, and Prince George County. These events are designed to provide accessible health services, connect residents to social support resources, and promote community resilience in underserved areas. Services include onsite immunizations, health screenings, food distribution, safety education, family activities, and vendor engagement. Each event is tailored to the specific needs of its host locality and aligned with identified health priorities.

Each event in the series is carefully planned using data driven insights from Crater Health District’s annual Community Health Assessments (CHA), which identify health priorities, resource gaps, and barriers to care unique to each locality. These assessments were conducted in partnership with community leaders and stakeholders and guided the selection of services and partners for each fair. For example, events in rural areas like Surry and Greensville/Emporia emphasized transportation access, substance use prevention, and chronic disease screenings, while urban events in Petersburg and Hopewell prioritized immunizations, housing support, and food insecurity. This targeted approach ensures that each event is not only accessible but also responsive to the community’s self-identified needs. For more information about the Crater Health District's Community Health Assessments, please visit the VDH Crater Health District website here: Visit the VDH Crater Health District Community Health Assessments Page

The key to a successful health fair is getting people through the door and offering services that resonate with them. By the end of this tutorial, you will be able to sucessfully host a resource fair in a health district of your own. Here are some strategies from the playbook:

Timeline

3-6 months before the event

  • Look at CHA Indicators
  • Identify possible partners, sponsors, and vendors.
  • Host discovery meeting with CHD Program Heads. This may take place during the preset of event dates.
  • Choose venue
  • Choose fun theme/event name (often seasonal)
  • Finalize healthcare providers and offerings
  • Secure permits an insurance (check with partners to access their capabilities/coverage)
  • Confirm staff availability and internal planning team
  • Develop communications/marketing plan and budget.
  • Establish bi-weekly meeting times with planning team and stakeholders.

1-3 months before the event

  • Establish key performance indicators and community engagement metrics.
  • Create vendor, volunteer, and transportation registration along with layout map
  • Identify giveaways, raffles, and incentives
  • Offer something unique: Grocery Giveaway, Blood Drive, Adopt a pet, and/or Kids Corner.
  • Confirm vendors, food trucks/free food, entertainment, and speakers/workshop presenters
  • Create event registration on Eventbrite (this will automatically be translated into Spanish)
  • Create Facebook event
  • Develop Marketing: save the date, flyers, mini promos, signage, and stamp cards.
  • Send calendar invites vendor and staff meetings as well as the event day.
  • Arrange transportation options (if applicable).
  • Schedule Print and Pack Day
  • Begin working on Event Plan
  • Begin working on Press Release

1-3 weeks prior to the event

  • Finalize logistics (tables, tents, power sources, walkies, vests, attire, medical dividers, sign holders, water/snacks/food, tablecloths, paperwork for screenings & vendor/staff check-in).
  • Review supply inventory sheet and needed contents for CHD Program tables
  • Print maps, schedules, stamp cards, and signage
  • Send out reminders to vendors and staff
  • Finalize Event Plan
  • Collect giveaways and raffles
  • Have Print and Pack Day
  • Have final vendor meeting during the week of the event

Day prior to the event

  • Host internal staff meeting to review roles & responsibilities, flow of event, attire, breaks, and event plan.
  • Setup day prior to event, if possible (tech, raffle prizes, talking points, backdrops, vendor area, breakroom, etc.).

Day of the event

  • Set up check-in areas, tents, tables, and signage
  • Distribute materials (maps, passports, event plan, and walkies)
  • Provide safety briefing 30 mins prior to start of event to all participating staff including vendors and volunteers. Take Group Photo before getting gross.
  • Conduct welcome announcements, thank sponsors, special guests, vendors, and attendees
  • Take photos and gather feedback from attendees
  • Breakdown

Post event

  • Send out post event surveys and thank you cards/emails
  • Staff, Vendor, Stakeholder hotwash (within a week of the event)
  • Analyze data collected by vendors
  • Create After Action Report (AAR) for event

Suggested Team Leads

  1. Project Manager/Event Lead – Appointed by Health Director
  2. Marketing Lead
  3. Outreach Lead
  4. Signage Lead
  5. Registration Lead
  6. Vendor Lead
  7. Staff Lead
  8. Volunteer Lead
  9. Stakeholder Lead
  10. Kids Lead
  11. Medical Lead
  12. Safety Lead
  13. Finance Lead
  14. Parking Lead
